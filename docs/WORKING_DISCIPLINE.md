# Working discipline and evidence

Use this guide for bounded repository maintenance or a production's material
decision, reported content-domain defect, or evidence-heavy final review. It
does not grant production, external service, configuration, data-operation, or
publishing authority. Routine content work follows the normal workflow.

For repository maintenance, use accepted inputs and supplied paths only. Do not
preload personal AIOS context, channel or learning material, or another
production. A real production follows its own input route in `AGENTS.md`.

## Judgment before change

Inspect actual constraints before making a material product, business, or trust
decision. Consider reasonable alternatives, then record context, rationale,
consequences, and supersession in the existing brief, cut plan, production
notes, graph, final review, or maintenance record nearest to the work. Ask only
when owner authority or a material decision is genuinely missing. Keep public
documentation current with changed behavior and each source-of-truth record's
owner and provenance.

For existing helper maintenance, preserve the small zero-dependency contract
and add comments only for non-obvious intent; do not turn content work into a
new runtime or media pipeline.

## Defects and proof

For a defect, reproduce the affected caller's behavior at the nearest safe,
representative boundary before changing it. Preserve expectations; do not delete,
skip, weaken, or narrow a check merely to obtain a passing result. Verify a
meaningful regression, or state why the original behavior could not be
reproduced and what representative evidence was used.

Editorial, graph, handoff, and design-direction defects use actual domain proof:
artifact review, graph or handoff validation, provenance/hash inspection, or a
bounded equivalent. Browser E2E or full production scaffolding is unnecessary
when it would not test the failure. A contract change records its rationale and
has replacement coverage proportionate to the altered behavior.

## Safety, final review, and handback

Never disclose or commit secrets, including real `.env` contents. Necessary,
approved private source media or data may remain only in its exact assigned
operational context, such as an ignored local artifact inside its production
boundary, with truthful non-sensitive provenance. Shared files, examples, and
fixtures use safe, synthetic data.

Final review checks the exact candidate bytes/hash and its owning record. Keep
per-node approval, the supervised not-posted handoff, and immutable design
direction intact. Hand back in the user's language and requested depth with
paths, hashes, checks, recovery state, and limitations; preserve unrelated work.
