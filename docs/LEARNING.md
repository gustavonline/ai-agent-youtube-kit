# Learning loop

Keep durable learning in transparent Markdown:

- `workspace/learning/PROJECT_MEMORY.md` for reusable production lessons;
- `workspace/channel/STYLE_GUIDE.md` for reference/performance-backed channel
  patterns;
- `workspace/references/REFERENCES.md` for analyzed references;
- `workspace/channel/DESIGN.md` for direction accepted by the design owner; and
- `workspace/learning/MOTION_PHILOSOPHY.md` for general code-motion principles.

Production-specific facts stay in that production's notes and review. Promote
only concrete, reusable lessons after a finished outcome. Do not maintain
hidden history state or a recovery ledger.

Diffusion remains the ordinary Studio. HyperFrames lessons belong here only when
a full specialist code-motion outcome or bounded overlay established a durable
principle.
