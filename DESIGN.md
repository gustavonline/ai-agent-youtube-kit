# Design System

This is the default brand direction for new AI agent YouTube videos. Replace it with your own brand once you know the channel identity.

## Style Prompt

Sharp, technical, credible, and fast. The videos should feel like a capable operator explaining useful automation systems, not a generic crypto or SaaS hype reel. Use dense information, clean hierarchy, crisp typography, and motion that clarifies the point.

## Colors

- Canvas: `#0A0D10`
- Surface: `#121821`
- Text: `#F4F7FA`
- Muted text: `#8FA3B8`
- Signal cyan: `#2EE7D2`
- Action amber: `#FFB020`
- Alert red: `#FF4D5E`

## Typography

- Primary: `Inter`
- Mono/data: `Menlo`, `SF Mono`, monospace

## Motion Rules

- Build the hero frame first, then animate into it.
- Use 1-2 strong moves per scene, not constant movement everywhere.
- Favor quick ease-out entrances and short holds.
- Motion should land on spoken keywords or major beat changes.
- Captions must remain readable on phone screens.

## What Not To Do

- No generic blue-purple gradients as the main identity.
- No tiny captions or dense paragraphs in a 9:16 frame.
- No animated decorations that compete with the speaker or key idea.
- No default brand names, placeholder logos, or borrowed AIS/Nate identity.
- No render handoff before visual inspection.
