## Operational flow

Use one row or note for the smallest useful sequence:

```text
core idea + intended sales job
  -> chosen capture format
  -> core video
  -> only enabled, channel-native harvests
  -> contextual CTA / supervised handoff
```

Start the idea bank with client questions or calls, mistakes, working proof,
mechanisms, philosophy, and current tests. Give the core video one job:
attention, nurture/proof, or conversion/next step. A YouTube/library or email
layer can carry long-form trust and the CTA while short-form, feed, or LinkedIn
harvests earn attention and relationship; choose only the routes enabled by
the clone policy. Do not make any one channel mandatory.

The nine capture formats remain available. Pick the format that makes the
available proof visible, then reformat the idea/copy for each selected channel
instead of blindly duplicating one video file.
