# Business Freedom content family r1

This production is a real, local, code-native content-family tracer:

- the reviewed 42-second 1920×1080 landscape master is preserved byte for byte;
- two distinct 24-second 1080×1920 Diffusion scenes adapt different parts of the story;
- a delivery-ready text post, reviewed 4:5 visual, and newsletter draft extend the family;
- every selected delivery node has its own approval, exact hash, provenance,
  and lineage in `content-graph.json`.

Gustav Online is the public-learning and Demand voice. onlinesourdough owns the
Offer / Operations / Demand diagnosis and offer menu. Resources is DIY, The
Fermentary is done with you, and Complete Bake is done for you; Arc’IT AI is
shown only inside Complete Bake in practice. AIOS routes bounded context,
owner, next action, and evidence. ADS, ACS, and Diffusion stay separately
owned. Business Freedom means fewer repeated founder decisions while Gustav
keeps the final choice.

## Delivery state

The graph-bound deliverables are ready for a supervised owner decision only.
`publisher-handoff.json` remains `awaiting-separate-authorization`, supervised,
not posted, and unable to post externally. A target is delivery intent, never
authority.

This package does not claim a measured business result, proven public outcome,
maximum automation, an installed monolith, or real owner-recorded footage.
Issue #12's real owner-video acceptance remains open and requires a separate
camera/screen/podcast source usability run.

## Local byte policy

Current Git policy ignores MP4 and VTT files. Their exact bytes remain inside
this production through Review and are bound by graph hashes. They are not
force-added and the ignore policy is unchanged; lead/owner decides media/VTT
delivery at Ship review.
