# Thesis

Business Freedom is not hands-off automation. For this founder, it means reducing the repeated visual, version, production-lineage, and approval decisions that keep useful Demand content waiting on Gustav—while preserving his final choice.

The smallest complete response is one explicitly owned path:

1. Gustav Online makes useful knowledge and cases public learning.
2. onlinesourdough diagnoses the business and selects Demand as the current constraint.
3. The offer menu chooses the delivery mode: Resources (DIY), The Fermentary (done with you), or Complete Bake (done for you), with Arc’IT AI visible only in Complete Bake practice.
4. AIOS routes bounded context, owner, next action, and evidence.
5. ADS contributes accepted reusable visual direction; ACS owns content lineage, independent review, and supervised handoff; Diffusion Studio renders the ACS-owned motion source locally.
6. A human keeps the approval decision. The handoff remains unposted.

These are modular brands, repositories, and products with separate ownership—not one installed stack. The film demonstrates a real reviewable production artifact, not a measured business result or proven public performance outcome.
