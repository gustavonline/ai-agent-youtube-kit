# ACS cut and storyboard plan

**Workspace/source(s):** Owner delegation, `content-brief.md`, accepted `design-input/DESIGN.md`, and the three selected immutable ADS keyframes
**Transcript:** `script.md` and `captions.vtt`; there is no recorded dialogue
**Target runtime:** preserved 42-second landscape master plus two 24-second vertical adaptations at 30 fps

## Editorial strategy

- Core story: one founder-owned Demand constraint becomes a locally rendered, reviewable, supervised handoff without removing human choice.
- What to remove: generic AI claims, logo parade, agent swarms, system-installation language, performance metrics, or any suggestion of automatic publishing.
- What to preserve: a single terracotta constraint line, separate ownership fields, honest offer roles, bounded AIOS proof, and the warm Business Freedom close.
- Pacing: eight hard 5.25-second beats; one decisive reveal per beat; each open caption remains visible for the whole beat.

## Risky transitions

| Location | Risk | Decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Offer menu → Complete Bake | Could make Arc’IT look like the umbrella brand | Change the whole field to sand/green only for this bounded beat; return to AIOS dark afterward |
| AIOS → ADS/ACS/Diffusion | Could imply one installed stack | Use separate panels, explicit owner verbs, and “separately owned” copy |
| Final review mark | Could imply publication or measured success | Pair the mark with `SUPERVISED · NOT POSTED` and no outcome metric |
| Code-native opener | ADS reference includes a portrait, but production forbids media nodes | Preserve human voice, orange field, direct copy, and the founder label; record the no-media limitation honestly |
