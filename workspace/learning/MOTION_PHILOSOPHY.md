# Motion philosophy

Diffusion is the ordinary Studio. Use upstream HeyGen HyperFrames only for an
entire code-animated explainer/motion-graphics video or one bounded overlay
asset. It is not another editor or a normal long-form route.

## Principles

1. Every scene has one job.
2. The viewer knows where to look before motion starts.
3. Text remains readable at phone size.
4. Motion explains, reveals, or emphasizes rather than decorates.
5. Design the strongest frame before animation.
6. Inspect rendered frames and the final export.
7. Register the reviewed output as its own graph node; approval never inherits.
